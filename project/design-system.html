Illustrations

Simple graphics for empty, error, success and connection states. Use EmptyState (illustration, title, body, optional action) or Illustration on its own from _ds/…/illustrations.js.

Style rules

  • Soft circular backdrop (r 60 on a 160 canvas), centered, with plenty of space around it.
  • 2px outlines in --illustration-line, object bodies in --illustration-surface.
  • One small filled area per drawing in --illustration-fill, with --illustration-detail for lines on top of it.
  • Flat 2D only: no 3D, shading, scenes, heavy gradients or decoration. Not used in auth or onboarding.

Tokens

--illustration-backdrop
→ blue-100, 9% to 0% opacity
--illustration-line
→ blue-100
--illustration-fill
→ blue-200
--illustration-surface
→ bg-primary
--illustration-detail
→ blue-50
